# Construct and source ledger

**Registry:** `EXP-DELIB` / `COMP-PAR-DELIB`  
**Design status:** `HUMAN_REVIEW`  
**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

The competency structures legitimate disagreement-resolution processes that clarify issues, interests, rights, evidence, options, authority, power, uncertainty, and possible agreements while preserving consent, inclusion, declared role, confidentiality, dissent, and reviewability. Debate, mediation, negotiation, adjudication, and public deliberation remain distinct.

## Release conditions

Human review is required for role/authority, sensitive scenarios, privacy, power, rights, jurisdiction, and consequential decisions. Use fictional low-stakes fixtures. Do not claim neutrality or force consensus.
